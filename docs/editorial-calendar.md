## Publication checklist

- MG-approved title, summary, and body
- Stable lowercase hyphenated slug
- Controlled category
- Named author
- Publication date
- Approved HTTPS cover image
- Descriptive cover-image alt text
- Related service selected in the admin editor
- Relevant in-body or footer service CTA
- Accuracy and claim review completed

The backend refuses publication until the author, related service, cover image, and cover-image alt text are present. Demo articles are available only in demo content mode and must not be treated as launch editorial content.
